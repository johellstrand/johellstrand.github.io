Chapter 01

Get started

WattPrice shows published wholesale electricity prices on iPhone, iPad and Apple Watch. Use it to compare electricity areas, inspect upcoming prices and find a cheaper time for a continuous task.

  1. Open WattPrice on your iPhone. Choose Find my electricity area, choose manually, or select Continue with SE1 for free.
  2. Confirm your electricity area. Check the area against your electricity contract or supplier’s information. Location is a suggestion and can be corrected.
  3. Choose how prices appear. Open Settings → Price display and select a currency and kWh or MWh.
  4. Let prices load. Read the current price, upcoming intervals and price outlook. Pull down on the main screen to check for updates.
What the price includes

App prices exclude VAT, electricity taxes, grid fees and supplier charges. They are not your total electricity bill. Negative wholesale prices are possible. Prices are published day-ahead data, not a forecast made by WattPrice.

SE1 in northernmost Sweden is the free preview. All other areas require the monthly subscription. You can use WattPrice without an Apple Watch or location permission.

Chapter 02

Choose and compare areas

Open Settings → Choose areas. Select up to 10 areas using the map or searchable list.

  • Map: tap an area to select or remove it. Selected polygons use the same colors as their chart lines.
  • Search all areas: search by country, zone code or EIC identifier. Only areas with supported map boundaries are selectable.
  • Selected-area chips: tap a chip to focus the map on that area. Fit selection brings all selected mapped areas into view.
  • Done: finish choosing areas. During first-time manual setup the picker stays open so you can select several.

If you reach the limit, remove an area before adding another. Selecting a paid area opens the subscription screen when access is locked. Removing an area hides it from the app; its downloaded prices may be reused if you select it again.

Using location

Location is requested only when you choose Find my electricity area. Matching happens on your device; your location is not saved or sent to the price service. If permission is denied, accuracy is insufficient or the area cannot be matched, choose manually or use SE1. The map starts over Europe even if you are elsewhere.

Choosing an Apple Watch area

When a Watch is paired, choose Use on Watch in the area details or selected-area list. iPhone compares all your selected areas; Watch displays one designated area. Removing the Watch area assigns the first remaining selected area. Removing all areas clears Watch setup. Watch controls appear only when a Watch is paired.

Chapter 03

Read the price screen

Each area’s price card is labeled with its name and code. Check the displayed unit and the interval’s start and end times when reading a value.

Price card guide
ItemMeaning
Current / NextThe current published interval, or the next available interval if there is no current one. Check its time range.
Next 3 pricesUp to three published intervals after the main displayed interval. Fewer appear when less future data is available.
Time until maximum / minimumTime until the highest or lowest current/upcoming published price. Now means that interval is active. Ties use the earliest interval.
Prices refreshedThe last successful price download for this area. An asterisk (*) marks a download by a background task or widget.

Intervals may be hourly or shorter. Adjacent intervals with the same formatted price can appear as one longer period. Calculations retain the original, unrounded prices. Gaps in publications remain gaps; missing prices are never treated as zero.

Expand a card to see additional area details. Supported Swedish, Norwegian and Danish areas can also show publisher Price summaries. These notices retain the publisher’s language, prices and tax wording, which may differ from the app’s wholesale-price display.

Chapter 04

Explore the price outlook

Price outlook uses stepped lines so each price lasts for its published interval. It shows equal spans of time before and after now, ending at the last published future interval.

  • Touch and drag across the chart to inspect a time and the corresponding prices below.
  • Tap an area row below the chart to show only that area. Tap it again, or choose Show all areas, to restore the comparison.
  • The time range stays fixed when focusing an area; the vertical price scale adjusts. This filter does not change selected areas or the Watch area.

With original currencies, areas using different currencies appear in separate charts. Choose a display currency to compare them in one currency. Fixed-contract comparison changes chart colors as explained in Fixed-price contracts.

Chapter 05

Find the cheapest time

Quick search in an area card

  1. Find the area’s Cheapest window section.
  2. Move the Duration slider from 1 to 180 minutes.
  3. Read the recommended start, end and average price. Start now means the best available window begins now.

The search considers continuous windows from now through the end of published coverage. It weights prices by the time spent in each interval and never bridges missing data. Equal-price results use the earliest start. If no complete window fits, shorten the duration or wait for more published prices.

A time window, not an appliance bill

The average assumes constant consumption throughout the window. WattPrice does not estimate appliance energy use or total running cost. For durations up to 48 hours and a finish deadline, use Shortcuts.

Chapter 06

Daily price briefing

Open Daily price briefing below the area cards. Choose a selected area and Today or Tomorrow.

  • Average and published coverage: a time-weighted average with the number of published hours. Partial days are labeled.
  • Cheapest remaining 2 hours: the best continuous two-hour window still available within the selected day.
  • Upcoming peak: the highest relevant upcoming published price period.
  • Change from previous day: available only when both days have complete, compatible published data.

The As of time anchors the remaining-day calculations. Pull to refresh to update it. If tomorrow has not been published, choose today or check later.

Summarize with AI optionally writes a short on-device summary of the calculated figures. The figures work without Apple Intelligence. Changing the inputs or leaving the screen clears the AI summary. The briefing does not schedule alerts.

Chapter 07

Currency, units and rounding

Open Settings → Price display. Choose original currencies or a display currency: SEK, EUR, USD, NOK, DKK, GBP, CHF, PLN or CZK. Choose kWh or MWh; one MWh equals 1,000 kWh.

For SEK/kWh you can enable Show öre/kWh. For EUR/kWh or USD/kWh, enable Show cents/kWh. One major currency unit equals 100 öre or cents.

The Decimal places slider runs from −2 to 4. Negative values round to tens or hundreds: 153.2341 becomes 150 at −1 or 200 at −2. Use Automatic to restore the default. Rounding affects labels, not calculations, and syncs to Watch and widgets.

Conversions use the latest available European Central Bank reference-rate publication, whose date is shown in the app. These are not live trading rates. If a required conversion rate is missing, prices fall back to original currencies per MWh with a notice. Check the unit again after a fallback.

App times follow the device’s time zone unless a view states otherwise. Scandinavian history uses Swedish time. Daily calculations account for daylight-saving days with 23 or 25 hours.

Chapter 08

Compare a fixed-price contract

  1. Open Settings → Fixed-price contract.
  2. Enable Compare with fixed price.
  3. Select the Contract currency and enter the energy rate per kWh in that currency, excluding taxes and fees. For example, enter 0.80 SEK for a rate of 80 öre/kWh.

The contract currency remains independent of your display currency. WattPrice converts the reference to match the chart’s currency and unit.

  • A dashed reference appears on iPhone and Watch price outlooks and the iPhone 365-day chart.
  • In a single-area chart, green means spot prices are above the fixed rate; red means they are at or below it. Multiple-area charts retain each area’s color.
  • Fixed price cheaper shows the share of available daily averages above the fixed rate. Equal prices do not count as cheaper.
  • Price difference balance compares the summed daily differences above and below the rate. Available days receive equal weight; missing days are excluded.

Tap fixed price in an iPhone chart legend to return to these settings. Chart values remain spot prices. These historical comparisons do not account for your actual consumption pattern or calculate personal savings. If conversion is unavailable, the app shows a notice.

Chapter 09

Use historical comparisons

Daily price comparison vs last year

Available for selected Swedish SE1–SE4, Norwegian NO1–NO5 and Danish DK1–DK2 areas. Expand the section to compare the last 30 completed days, ending yesterday, with the same calendar dates one year earlier. Choose Show 30 more days to extend the period.

Use Comparison to select Prices, Difference or Difference (%). In Prices mode, solid lines show the recent period and dashed lines last year. Difference is recent minus last year; percentage divides that difference by the absolute value of last year’s price. A zero baseline has no percentage and shows “—”. Very large outliers may trigger a labeled symmetric logarithmic scale.

Drag to inspect dates and values. Period averages stay fixed while you inspect individual days. Tap an area row to focus it; use Show all areas to restore all areas. Missing dates leave gaps. Initial history downloads can take time; progress is shown and downloaded daily averages are saved.

Daily prices · Last 365 days

Expand this section for Swedish, Norwegian, Danish and Finnish areas to see daily averages including today, extrema and area summaries. Today can still be incomplete. Drag to inspect a day and use the area rows to focus the chart. Fixed-contract summaries appear when configured. These Nordic history sections follow Swedish calendar days and the app’s display preferences.

Daily price vs 5 year ATH

Open this screen and choose a selected area. The teal line shows the last 30 completed days; the dashed orange line shows each matching calendar date’s highest complete daily average across the current window and four earlier yearly windows. Drag to see the historical date that set each daily high.

“ATH” here means a high within those five matching windows, not an all-time record across every date. Only complete EUR daily averages are included. This view uses original EUR/MWh values and two decimals independently of display preferences, even though its chart omits unit labels. Coverage counts show how much data is available; records can change as missing data loads. Use Try again after an incomplete load.

Chapter 10

Set up price notifications

After you select areas, WattPrice requests notification permission on a foreground refresh. Open Settings → Price notifications to turn alerts on or off. If permission was denied, use the link to iOS Settings.

At 17:00 in your iPhone’s local time, one summary per selected, accessible area compares today’s average with tomorrow’s and includes tomorrow’s lowest and highest prices with their times.

  • Both days must have complete downloaded prices. A missing publication does not produce a summary, and a missed 17:00 summary is not sent late.
  • There are no separate notifications when the lowest or highest price begins.
  • Once scheduled, alerts are delivered locally by iOS. Future summaries still require new downloaded prices.
  • Focus modes and system notification settings control presentation. Disabling price notifications removes pending price alerts.

If summaries stop, open WattPrice to load tomorrow’s prices and check notification permission and Background App Refresh.

Chapter 11

Add iPhone and iPad widgets

  1. Open WattPrice once and select areas so prices and preferences are saved.
  2. Open your Home Screen widget gallery and choose WattPrice → Electricity price.
  3. Add a size, then use Edit Widget → Electricity area to choose an area already selected in the app.

Each widget can use a different selected area. The default is the first selected phone area. If you remove an explicitly chosen area from WattPrice, reconfigure its widget. Tap a widget to open the app.

Widget colors show the relative price range displayed: green is the lowest, yellow the midpoint and red the highest; flat prices use yellow. They do not indicate an absolute cheap/expensive threshold. Widgets follow the app’s currency, unit and rounding preferences. iOS controls background refresh timing.

Chapter 12

Use Apple Watch

  1. Pair your Watch with iPhone. In Apple’s Watch app, install WattPrice from Available Apps if needed.
  2. In WattPrice on iPhone, open Settings → Choose areas and mark a selected area with Use on Watch.
  3. Keep the devices connected and open WattPrice on both so settings can sync.

Swipe vertically or turn the Digital Crown to move between the current-price page, chart, maximum countdown and minimum countdown. The current-price page includes the next three prices. Longer content scrolls within a page.

The chart shades past time and shows stepped prices, price guides and a time-weighted average of current and published future prices. Watch uses the iPhone’s display preferences and fixed-price reference. Once configured, it downloads prices independently; setup and area selection remain on iPhone.

Add a watch-face complication

Edit a compatible watch face and choose WattPrice in a circular or rectangular slot. Open the Watch app once before configuring it.

  • Electricity price · Circular: current price and a gauge within the remaining published range.
  • Electricity price · Rectangular: current price, area, upcoming chart and lowest-price countdown.
  • Next 3 prices · Rectangular: current interval and three upcoming start times and prices.
  • Peak price warning · Rectangular: a warning during the first period at the local day’s highest published price, including the peak end time.

Tap a complication to open WattPrice. All Watch layouts use the designated Watch area. Enable Widget Suggestions on Watch to allow low-price and peak-price suggestions; watchOS decides when to display them. A complication warning is distinct from an iPhone notification.

Check Settings → App info → Apple Watch on iPhone for pairing, installation, connection and configured area. A temporary disconnection queues settings for later syncing.

Chapter 13

Use Siri and Shortcuts

Open WattPrice and choose areas first. In Apple’s Shortcuts app, add one of these WattPrice actions:

WattPrice actions
ActionHow to use it
Get PriceChoose an area and optional date/time; leave Time empty for now. Returns a numeric price, currency, unit, interval and cache update time.
Find cheapest electricity windowChoose an area, duration in hours and optional Finish before deadline. Fractional hours are supported, up to 48 hours. Defaults to two hours within the next 24 hours.
Open electricity areaOpens WattPrice and scrolls to the chosen area without changing selected areas or the Watch area.

An omitted area uses the first selected iPhone area. Removed areas return an error. Price requests outside published coverage or in a gap also fail explicitly. Results follow your display settings and use downloaded published prices.

Try “Get the electricity price with WattPrice” or “Find cheap electricity with WattPrice.” Shortcut phrases currently use English; recognition depends on Siri’s language. Actions can also be assigned through Shortcuts to the Action button on supported devices.

Chapter 14

Power generation and flows

Expand Power Generation and Flows near the end of the main screen and choose a selected area.

  • Produced, Imported, Exported: reported energy since local midnight through the latest included time. Totals use MWh or GWh; incomplete data is labeled.
  • Generation mix: reported local generation by type. Shares exclude imports and storage consumption and are not the mix of electricity you personally consume.
  • Wind and solar outlook: published day-ahead generation forecasts in MW.
  • Imports and exports: net physical flow for listed borders, not a complete area balance.

Coverage and delays vary by area. Missing reports are not counted as zero, and unpublished periods are not extrapolated. This is reported energy context, not live measurement or a price forecast.

Data loads while the section is expanded and the app is active. Use Refresh generation and flow data or pull to refresh while expanded. Normal refresh intervals are 15 minutes for generation/flows and one hour for forecasts.

Chapter 15

Updates, offline use and privacy

When prices update

WattPrice normally checks for new prices at 13:37 device-local time on the last day covered by the saved publication. Missing or expired coverage is fetched immediately. While active, due downloads retry after five minutes if they fail or return unchanged data. Pull-to-refresh checks under this policy; it does not force a new download before one is due.

Downloaded future intervals keep advancing offline until their coverage ends. After expiry, missing data is shown rather than carrying an old price forward. iOS and watchOS control actual background execution. Background App Refresh, Low Power Mode and force-quitting can affect updates.

Clear downloaded data

Settings → Clear cache removes downloaded prices, history, exchange rates, energy-context data and the area catalogue on that device, then fetches fresh data. It keeps your selected areas, settings and network statistics.

Settings → App info shows version, device details, Watch status and network transfer totals. Its trash button clears network statistics separately.

Your area choices, downloaded prices and display settings are saved on the device. Relevant Watch settings and access information sync to the paired Watch. Location matching and optional AI processing happen on-device. See the privacy policy for details.

Chapter 16

Subscriptions and support

SE1 is free. One auto-renewable monthly subscription unlocks all other areas. The App Store price appears before purchase. Open subscription access in Settings or select a locked area to see purchase options.

  • Restore purchases: use the subscription screen with the Apple Account used for the purchase.
  • Manage or cancel: use subscription management in WattPrice or open iPhone system Settings → your name → Subscriptions.
  • After cancellation: access continues until the paid period ends.
  • Watch after renewal: open WattPrice on iPhone and reconnect your paired Watch to sync updated access.

Widgets and complications may show Subscription required, Subscribe or Open app to check subscription when paid-area access needs attention. No data indicates missing prices with valid access. SE1 remains free.

Support the developer at the bottom of the main screen offers an optional one-time tip. Tips do not unlock subscription features.

For help, email iosappsupport@icloud.com. Include your WattPrice version from App info, device and system version, selected area, and what happened. The support page has more help.
