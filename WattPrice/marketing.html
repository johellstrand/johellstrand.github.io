Go a little further

Build prices into your routine.

Find your cheapest window

Use WattPrice in Apple’s Shortcuts app to search for the cheapest published window for a chosen duration and optional finish deadline. A useful starting point for deciding when to run flexible energy tasks.

Other actions retrieve a price for a published time or open a selected electricity area. Add them to your own workflows or use supported Siri phrases.

Follow market updates

Read recent Nord Pool market messages for supported selected areas and open the original notices for details.

The feed includes published updates, revisions and cancellations. It provides context rather than a complete inventory of active outages or push alerts.

Available in English, Swedish, German, French, Danish, Finnish, Norwegian Bokmål, Spanish and Italian.
