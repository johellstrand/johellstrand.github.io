Privacy policy

How WattPrice handles information when you use the app, its widgets, Apple Watch companion and this website.

Effective and last updated: 12 November 2026

Privacy at a glance

  • No WattPrice account is required.
  • WattPrice has no advertising, third-party analytics SDKs or advertising tracking.
  • Your area selections, preferences and downloaded prices are stored on your devices.
  • Optional location-based area matching happens on your iPhone. The app does not send your coordinates to its electricity price providers.
  • Online services receive the network requests needed to supply prices, maps, market messages and purchases.
  • If you email support, we receive your email address and the information you choose to send.

1. Who is responsible

WattPrice (“we”, “us” or “our”) is responsible for the personal information it processes as described in this policy. This policy covers the WattPrice iPhone app, Apple Watch app, widgets, complications, Shortcuts actions and WattPrice pages hosted on this website.

For privacy questions or requests, contact iosappsupport@icloud.com. Use “WattPrice Privacy” as the subject so we can identify your request.

2. Information used by the app

Settings and downloaded information

WattPrice stores selected electricity areas, your designated Watch area, currency and unit preferences, display settings, downloaded prices, exchange rates, the area catalogue and refresh-related timestamps on your devices. These support price display, offline use and updates. Deselecting an area does not necessarily erase its cached prices.

WattPrice does not read your electricity meter, electricity bill, supplier account, home energy consumption or household appliance activity.

Optional location

If you grant location permission, WattPrice requests a location to suggest an electricity area during initial setup. Area matching uses boundaries stored on your iPhone. Coordinates used for this matching are not saved by WattPrice or sent to ENTSO-E, the ECB, Nord Pool or a developer-operated server. The resulting selected electricity area is saved and used in area-specific network requests.

The area map uses Apple MapKit, including a current-location control and location marker when permitted. Apple may process map requests and location-related information when these features are used, as described in Apple Maps & Privacy. WattPrice does not maintain a location history or use continuous background location tracking.

Purchases

Apple processes subscription purchases. WattPrice uses verified StoreKit transaction and subscription information to determine access, restore purchases and handle renewal, expiry, refunds and revocation. The subscription expiry is stored on-device and shared with your paired Apple Watch. WattPrice does not receive your payment card details or Apple Account password and does not upload your purchase history to a developer-operated server.

Device sharing and Shortcuts

Your Watch area, display preferences, subscription expiry and service configuration sync to your paired Apple Watch through Apple’s WatchConnectivity framework. App extensions access relevant settings and cached data through Apple’s shared app storage. Service credentials, where applicable, are held in Apple Keychain and used only for the relevant price service and paired-device functionality.

Shortcuts actions return the prices, areas, times and other result fields you request to Apple’s Shortcuts system. If you configure a shortcut to pass those results to another app or service, that additional sharing is controlled by your shortcut.

3. Support correspondence

If you email us, we receive your sender address, any name included by your email service, your message, attachments and email metadata. We use this information to respond, investigate the issue, follow up and maintain a record of the support provided. Sending device details or screenshots is optional. Please omit sensitive or unrelated information, passwords and payment details.

Support is handled through our iCloud email account. Information may be disclosed where required by law or necessary to establish, exercise or defend legal claims. We limit any such disclosure to what is required.

4. Retention and deletion

  • Device data: settings and downloaded data remain on your devices until changed, overwritten or removed. There is no fixed automatic deletion period for all caches. Removing an area may leave its cached data available for reuse.
  • App removal: deleting the app removes its ordinary local app data, subject to Apple’s handling of shared storage, extensions and backups. Remove the companion app from Apple Watch separately. Offloading an app can preserve its data.
  • Secure storage and backups: Keychain items can persist after app deletion. If you entered service credentials, use the relevant removal control in the app before deleting it. Copies retained in device or cloud backups are managed through your Apple backup settings.
  • Support emails and attachments: we delete these within 24 months after the support matter is resolved. If records must be retained for a legal obligation or an ongoing dispute, we keep only the necessary information for that purpose and delete it when that need ends.
  • Diagnostic reports: reports made available to us through Apple are used for troubleshooting and retained only as needed to investigate or resolve the issue, subject to Apple’s availability and retention settings.
  • Provider records: Apple’s purchase records and independent service providers’ logs are governed by their own retention policies. We cannot erase records held independently by those services.

You can request earlier deletion of support information by email. We will delete information we control unless a lawful exception requires retention, and explain any applicable exception. We cannot remotely erase information stored only on your devices.

5. Your choices and rights

  • Location: decline permission or revoke it in iPhone Settings → Privacy & Security → Location Services → WattPrice. You can select electricity areas manually; permission is not a condition of paid access.
  • Preferences: change areas, display settings and the designated Watch area in WattPrice Settings.
  • Purchases: manage or cancel renewal through Apple’s subscription settings. Deleting WattPrice does not cancel a subscription.
  • Apple diagnostics: manage optional system diagnostic sharing in your device’s Analytics & Improvements settings.
  • Support information: email us to request access, correction or deletion of personal information held in support correspondence.

Depending on your location and applicable law, you may have rights to access, correct, erase or obtain a portable copy of your personal information, restrict processing, object to processing based on legitimate interests, and withdraw consent without affecting earlier lawful processing. We do not make decisions with legal or similarly significant effects using solely automated processing.

Contact iosappsupport@icloud.com to exercise your rights. We may request information reasonably necessary to verify that the request concerns you. We respond within applicable legal deadlines, normally within one month for GDPR requests, and explain any permitted extension.

You may complain to your local data protection authority. In Sweden, this is Integritetsskyddsmyndigheten (IMY).

6. Legal bases and international processing

Where the GDPR applies, optional device location access relies on your consent. Processing needed to deliver app functionality and subscription access relies on performance of our agreement with you. Handling support requests, resolving technical faults and protecting the service relies on our legitimate interests in supporting users and maintaining a reliable app, balanced against your rights. Processing required by law relies on the relevant legal obligation.

External service providers may process information outside your country, including outside the European Economic Area. Where we transfer personal information to a provider acting on our behalf, we require a lawful transfer mechanism when applicable, such as an adequacy decision or approved standard contractual clauses with any necessary additional safeguards. You can contact us for information about safeguards relevant to information we control. Independent providers explain their transfers in their own privacy notices.

7. Security

WattPrice uses HTTPS for its data-provider connections and Apple’s storage and device security mechanisms, including Keychain for applicable service credentials. Access to support information is limited to handling support, privacy requests and related obligations. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure; avoid sending sensitive information in support messages.

8. Children’s privacy

WattPrice is a general-purpose electricity information app and is not designed to solicit personal information from children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children through support without any consent required by law. If you believe a child has sent personal information that should be removed, contact us so we can investigate and delete it where required.

9. Changes to this policy

We will update this page when WattPrice’s privacy practices change and revise the date above. Where required, material changes will be brought to your attention in the app or through another appropriate notice, and consent will be obtained before any new processing that requires it. An update to this policy does not itself constitute consent.
