Get started

1

Choose your area

Open Settings → Choose areas on iPhone. Select areas on the map or use Search all areas. Check that your area matches your electricity connection.

2

Set your display

In Settings → Price display, choose your currency, energy unit and decimal places. Compare selected areas in the iPhone charts.

3

Set up your Watch

Choose Use on Watch for an area on iPhone. Install and open WattPrice on your paired Apple Watch to receive the area and display settings.

Common questions

Why are prices missing or tomorrow’s prices unavailable?

WattPrice shows day-ahead prices published by ENTSO-E. Future prices appear only when published and available from the source. Some listed areas may not provide price data.

Check your internet connection, confirm the selected area and open the app to allow a refresh. Previously downloaded prices work offline for the periods they cover. Missing data does not mean the price is zero.

Why does the price differ from my electricity bill?

WattPrice shows wholesale electricity prices, excluding VAT, electricity taxes, grid fees and supplier charges. Your contract and electricity provider determine the amount you pay. Negative wholesale prices are possible.

How do currencies, units and times work?

Choose your display preferences in Settings → Price display. Currency conversion uses European Central Bank reference rates rather than live exchange rates. If a required rate is unavailable, the app shows original currencies per MWh with a notice.

1 MWh equals 1,000 kWh. Times follow your device’s time zone. Price intervals may be hourly or shorter. Lowest and highest price countdowns cover the current interval and published future intervals.

My Apple Watch has the wrong area or shows setup instructions.

On iPhone, open Settings → Choose areas and mark your preferred area with Use on Watch. Keep your paired devices connected and open WattPrice on both devices so settings can sync. The Watch displays one designated area, while iPhone can compare multiple areas.

Display preferences and subscription access also sync from iPhone. After changing settings or renewing a subscription, reconnect the devices and open the apps.

How do I add or update widgets and complications?

Add WattPrice’s Electricity price widget through your iPhone’s widget gallery. To choose an area, edit the widget and select an area already selected in WattPrice.

On Apple Watch, edit a compatible watch face and add WattPrice in a circular or rectangular complication slot. Open the Watch app once to receive settings.

If data is missing, open the relevant app with an internet connection. Widgets and complications use downloaded prices; iOS and watchOS control background refresh timing. If you removed a widget’s selected area, edit the widget to choose another one.

How do I find the cheapest time with Shortcuts?

After opening WattPrice and choosing areas, add the Find cheapest electricity window action in Apple’s Shortcuts app. Choose an area, duration and optional finish deadline. The action searches published prices only, so enough price data must be available for the requested duration.

You can also use Get Price to retrieve a price for a published time, or Open electricity area to open an area in WattPrice.

Why are there no market messages for my area?

Market messages come from Nord Pool’s public feed and are filtered to supported selected areas. Areas without a matching feed are identified in the app. This is a feed of recent published updates, including revisions and cancellations, rather than a complete list of active outages. WattPrice does not send push alerts for these messages.

Does WattPrice need my location?

Location access is optional and is used once to suggest an electricity area. Matching happens on your device; the location is not stored or sent to the price service. You can choose or correct your area manually in Settings.

Subscriptions and purchases

Sweden’s SE1 area is available as a free preview. All other areas require the auto-renewable monthly subscription. The current price is shown in the app before purchase.

  • Restore access: use Restore purchases in WattPrice’s subscription screen with the Apple Account used for the purchase.
  • Manage or cancel: use WattPrice’s subscription management option or open the Settings app on iPhone, tap your name, then Subscriptions.
  • After cancellation: access continues through the paid period. Cancelling renewal does not immediately end access.
  • Watch access: after a purchase or renewal, open WattPrice on iPhone and reconnect your paired Watch so updated access can sync.

For a refund request, visit Apple’s Report a Problem service. For app access issues, email WattPrice support.

About the data

Electricity prices come from the ENTSO-E Transparency Platform. Currency conversion uses European Central Bank reference rates. WattPrice shows published prices rather than forecasts. For more information, open Settings → About WattPrice in the app.
